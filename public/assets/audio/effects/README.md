# Sound Effects

Place your UI sound effect files here.

## Required Files
- **star-click.mp3** - Soft twinkle sound when clicking stars (0.3s)
- **memory-unlock.mp3** - Gentle chime when unlocking a memory (0.5s)
- **gallery-open.mp3** - Soft whoosh when opening gallery (0.2s)
- **all-unlocked.mp3** - Celebration sound when all memories unlocked (1s)

## Specifications
- **Format**: MP3 or OGG
- **Duration**: 0.1-1 second
- **File Size**: < 50KB each
- **Bitrate**: 64kbps
- **Volume**: Mixed at -15dB (subtle, not jarring)

## Free Sound Effect Sources
1. **Freesound** - https://freesound.org/
2. **Zapsplat** - https://www.zapsplat.com/
3. **Mixkit** - https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/

## Search Terms
- "soft chime"

## Tips
- Keep effects short and subtle
- Avoid harsh or loud sounds
- Test volume levels in the app
- Ensure effects complement the ambient music
